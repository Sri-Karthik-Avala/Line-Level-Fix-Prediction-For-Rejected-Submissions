 Problem Statement Nukes has an integer that can be represented as the bitwise OR of one or more integers between A and B (inclusive).
How many possible candidates of the value of Nukes's integer there are? 
 
 
 
 
 Constraints 
 1 ≤ A ≤ B < 2^{60} 
 A and B are integers. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Input The input is given from Standard Input in the following format: 
 A 
 B 
 
 
 
 
 
 Output Print the number of possible candidates of the value of Nukes's integer.

9
 
 
 
 
 
 Sample Output 1 4
 
 In this case, A=7 and B=9 . There are four integers that can be represented as the bitwise OR of a non-empty subset of { 7 , 8 , 9 }: 7 , 8 , 9 and 15 .
