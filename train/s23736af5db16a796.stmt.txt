Maximum Sum Sequence 

 
Given a sequence of numbers a 1 , a 2 , a 3 , ..., a n , find the maximum sum of a contiguous subsequence of those numbers. Note that, a subsequence of one element is also a contiquous subsequence.
 

 Input 
 
The input consists of multiple datasets. Each data set consists of:

 
 n 
 a 1 
 a 2 
.
.
 a n 
 

 
You can assume that 1 &le; n &le; 5000 and -100000 &le; a i &le; 100000.
 

 
The input end with a line consisting of a single 0.
 

 Output 

 
For each dataset, print the maximum sum in a line.
