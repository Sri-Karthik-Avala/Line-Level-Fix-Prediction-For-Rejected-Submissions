 Problem Statement You are given a string S consisting of letters b , d , p and q .
Determine whether S is a mirror string . 
 Here, a mirror string is a string S such that the following sequence of operations on S results in the same string S : 
 
 
 Reverse the order of the characters in S . 
 
 
 Replace each occurrence of b by d , d by b , p by q , and q by p , simultaneously. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Constraints 
 1 \leq |S| \leq 10^5 
 S consists of letters b , d , p , and q . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Input The input is given from Standard Input in the following format: 
 S 
 
 
 
 
 
 Output If S is a mirror string, print Yes . Otherwise, print No . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Sample Input 1 pdbq
 
 
 
 
 
 Sample Output 1 Yes
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Sample Input 2 ppqb
 
 
 
 
 
 Sample Output 2 No
